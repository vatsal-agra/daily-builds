## Honest assessment

This is a ~0.9M-parameter model trained for 1,600 steps on 170KB of text. It reliably produces English word shapes, spacing, capitalization after sentence-ending punctuation, and quoted-dialogue punctuation because those patterns are extremely frequent and low-entropy in the corpus. It does **not** reliably produce globally coherent narrative -- that needs far more parameters and far more data than a CPU-trained toy model has access to. The loss curve and perplexity above are the honest measure of where it landed, not the cherry-picked samples.
