Mars Rovers and the Search for Ancient Water

NASA's Perseverance rover landed in Jezero Crater in 2021, a site chosen because it once held a lake fed by a river delta. Its predecessor Curiosity had already found mineral evidence of long-lived water on Mars in Gale Crater. Perseverance carries a coring drill that seals rock samples into sealed tubes, left on the surface for a future sample-return mission to retrieve. Its Ingenuity helicopter proved that powered flight is possible in the thin Martian atmosphere, completing dozens of flights far beyond its planned five-flight demonstration. Rovers move slowly, a few hundred meters a day, because every drive is planned around avoiding rocks and slopes that could damage the wheels, as happened to Curiosity earlier in its mission.
