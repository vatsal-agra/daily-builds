Method. PSNR is (6·Y+U+V)/8 averaged over frames; bitrate is the total stream size over the clip duration. libx264 runs through ffmpeg at constant QP with the same GOP (30), then is decoded back and measured by the same code. BD-rate fits a cubic through log-rate vs PSNR over the overlapping range (4+ points).
