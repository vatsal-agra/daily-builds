Mira Ashgrove kept every wrong map she had ever drawn, pinned in rows along the tower wall, because a wrong map still told you something true about the year it was drawn in. Warden Kest brought her news from the Long Bridge, where Old Ren counted shapes crossing in the mist before dawn. The bridge was the only way over the gorge, and the gorge was the only thing standing between the valley and the grey. "Edges first," Kest wrote in his ledger. "Always ask why the edges." Mira sharpened her pen and began again.
