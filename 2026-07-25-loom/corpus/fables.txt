So it set out one grey morning, certain the day would go its way. But a storm was rolling in from the west, and the mouse's plan came apart before it had truly begun. Luck, or something like it, sent an unexpected ally down that same path at that same hour. The mouse never forgot that day, nor the help it very nearly refused out of pride. And that is why, to this day, the animals of the river bend say: "Pride climbs fastest and falls hardest."
