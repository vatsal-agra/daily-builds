About this run

Every heatmap here is the model's actual softmax(QK^T/√d) output captured live during autoregressive sampling — nothing is faked or re-simulated for display.

Reading the grid

Row i, column j: how much token i (query) attends to token j (key) when predicting what comes after position i. Because generation is causal, token i can only see j ≤ i — the upper-right triangle of every grid is always exactly zero.

Step through frames to watch attention patterns shift as new tokens are generated — some heads specialize in "attend to the immediately previous token," others spread across whole words or punctuation.
